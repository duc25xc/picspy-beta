# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese designers, AI artists, and content creators (18–35) who work on AI-generated images and video. They open PicSpy at night on a laptop — hunting for prompt inspiration mid-project, copying workflow configs, or posting their own work to earn passive income. Their context is creative momentum: they have a deadline or a spark, not patience for friction. Secondary users are agencies and AI studios who buy access for teams.

## Product Purpose

PicSpy is a prompt library and creator economy for AI-generated visuals. Users discover high-quality AI image workflows (prompts, seeds, parameters), copy them in one click, and share their own to earn token-based income. Success looks like a creator's first payout and a consumer's first "that's the exact workflow I needed."

## Brand Personality

Vivid, Generative, Rewarding. The voice is confident without being corporate: it knows this niche, respects the craft, and makes earning feel earned. Not hype, not hustle-culture, not cold-tool. Closer to a curated gallery that also pays you.

## Anti-references

- Replicate.com — sterile API-dashboard energy, no community
- Shutterstock — legacy stock site feel, no creator identity
- OpenSea (2021 era) — speculative clutter, distrust, NFT baggage
- Generic SaaS landing pages — hero-metric template, gradient-text headlines, identical icon-card grids
- MidJourney's Discord UI — chaotic, hard to browse, no hierarchy

## Design Principles

1. **Show the work first.** Every surface leads with the prompt output (the image), not the feature list.
2. **Reward is visible.** Token balances, earnings, and plan benefits are always one glance away — never buried.
3. **Dark for focus.** Creative tools used at night in dim rooms belong in the dark. Not dark as fashion, dark as function.
4. **Speed over ceremony.** One-click copy, instant preview, no confirmation dialogs where none are needed.
5. **Craft signals quality.** A polished interface communicates that the prompts inside are worth paying for.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Vietnamese as primary language; English UI strings acceptable where established. Reduce-motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Touch targets 44×44px minimum (mobile-first).
